About the clinic

Jaipur's trusted choice for modern, comfortable dentistry.

Thaper Dental Clinic is a dental practice on Sahakar Marg, Lalkothi, for people who want excellent treatment without the anxiety that usually comes with a dental visit.

We listen first, explain what we find, and agree on a treatment plan with you before we begin. Every decision, from the procedure to the schedule, is made with you, never for you.

Experienced Care

Careful diagnosis and treatment, with every option explained plainly.

State-of-the-art Hygiene

Strict sterilisation routines and a spotless, well-kept clinic.

Patient Comfort First

Gentle technique and a patient pace at every step.

Our services

Complete dental care, under one roof.

Whether it's a routine check-up or a complete smile makeover, here is what we offer and who it's for.

01 · Everyday care

General & Preventive Dentistry

The foundation of a healthy mouth. Regular care that catches small problems early, before they turn painful or expensive.

  • Check-up and consultation
  • Professional cleaning (scaling)
  • Fillings and cavity treatment
  • Gum care

Good for: routine visits, toothache, bleeding gums

02 · Your smile

Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Design

Natural-looking improvements planned around your face, your teeth and what you want to change, never an over-done "one-size" smile.

  • Smile design consultation
  • Shape, size and colour correction
  • Fixing chips, gaps and stains

Good for: weddings, confidence, uneven or discoloured teeth

03 · Save your tooth

Root Canal & Restorative Care

Comfort-focused treatment for deep decay, infection and damaged teeth, aimed at keeping your natural tooth wherever possible.

  • Root canal treatment
  • Crowns and caps
  • Restoring broken or worn teeth

Good for: severe toothache, sensitivity, cracked teeth

04 · Bright & straight

Teeth Whitening & Orthodontics

Brighter shade and better alignment, with treatment options matched to your age, goals and lifestyle.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Braces and aligner options
  • Retainers and follow-up

Good for: crooked teeth, stained teeth, teens and adults

05 · For the family

Dental Implants & Pediatric Care

Long-lasting replacement for missing teeth, plus gentle, friendly care that helps children build healthy habits early.

  • Implant consultation and planning
  • Replacing one or more missing teeth
  • Child-friendly check-ups

Good for: missing teeth, first dental visits for kids
